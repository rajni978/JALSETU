# JALSETU

JalSetu is a community-powered water intelligence and early-warning
platform designed to help communities and local governments identify
water stress before it becomes a crisis.

## Problem

Many communities face declining groundwater, irregular rainfall,
drying water bodies, increasing agricultural water demand and damaged
water infrastructure. However, relevant information is often scattered
across different sources.

## Our Solution

JalSetu combines environmental data and community observations to:

- Identify emerging water stress
- Visualize water-related risks
- Understand contributing factors
- Allow communities to report local water problems
- Help Panchayats prioritize interventions

## Core Approach

Collect → Analyze → Assess Risk → Prioritize → Act
